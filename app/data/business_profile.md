# Plover Payments (fictional business)

- What it sells: merchant accounts and card processing for US small businesses: countertop and mobile card terminals, a POS system for restaurants and retail, and an online payment gateway for e-commerce stores.
- Who it serves: US-based small businesses with under $2M in annual card sales: retail shops, restaurants and cafes, salons and spas, home services, and ecommerce stores.
- Who it does not serve: high-risk merchants (CBD, vape, firearms, adult, gambling, crypto), businesses outside the US, consumers, and enterprises.
- What it does not sell: loans or merchant cash advances, payroll, P2P transfers, crypto payments, and free processing.
- Pricing: interchange-plus with a monthly fee; terminals bought or leased; next-day funding.
- Competitors: Square, Stripe, Clover, Toast, PayPal.
- Campaign goal: leads (application form or sales call) from small businesses ready to switch or set up card processing.
- Campaign judged: Plover Generic Search, the account's non-brand search campaign. The account has no competitor campaign. Every decision is for this campaign only.
